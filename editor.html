How saving works
  1. Change anything below. Your edits are kept in this browser while you work.
  2. Click Preview site to check it.
  3. Click Download content.js, then upload it to your GitHub repo (replace the old one).
  4. Pictures and videos: upload them into the images folder on GitHub, then type the path here, like images/house.jpg or images/turntable.mp4. YouTube links work too.
